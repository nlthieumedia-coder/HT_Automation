HT_FINDER - CAI DAT MOT CLICK

Yeu cau: Windows x64, Premiere Pro 26.2 tro len.

1. Giai nen toan bo file HT_Finder_Setup_Windows.zip.
2. Dong hoan toan Premiere Pro.
3. Chay cong_cu\cai_dat\CAI_DAT_MOT_CLICK.bat.
4. Chap nhan quyen Administrator.
5. Mo Premiere Pro > Window > UXP Plugins > HT_Finder.

Bo cai tu dong cai UXP, yt-dlp va Bridge tai cong 19889. Bridge se tu khoi dong cung Windows.
De sua chua, chay SUA_CHUA.bat. De go cai dat, chay GO_CAI_DAT.bat.
