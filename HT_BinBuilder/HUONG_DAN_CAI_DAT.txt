HT_BINBUILDER 1.0.0 - HUONG DAN CAI DAT

Yeu cau:
- Windows x64
- Adobe Premiere Pro 26.2 tro len
- Dong Premiere Pro truoc khi cai dat

Cai dat:
1. Giai nen toan bo HT_BinBuilder_Setup_Windows.zip.
2. Mo cong_cu\cai_dat.
3. Bam dup CAI_DAT_MOT_CLICK.bat.
4. Mo Premiere Pro > Window > UXP Plugins > HT_BinBuilder.

Khong can Creative Cloud Desktop, Node.js hay UXP Developer Tool.
Neu plugin khong xuat hien, dong Premiere va chay SUA_CHUA.bat.
De go cai dat, chay GO_CAI_DAT.bat.
