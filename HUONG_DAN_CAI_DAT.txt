HT_AUTOMATION 4.0.0 - HUONG DAN CAI DAT WINDOWS X64
====================================================

Yeu cau:
- Windows x64.
- Adobe Creative Cloud Desktop.
- Adobe Premiere Pro 26.2 tro len.

Cai dat:
1. Dong Adobe Premiere Pro neu dang mo.
2. Nhan dup file com.hieuyt.htautomation_premierepro.ccx.
3. Chon Install trong Adobe Creative Cloud Desktop.
4. Chap nhan quyen Administrator neu Windows yeu cau.
5. Mo Premiere Pro va mo panel HT_Automation.

FFmpeg va native addon da nam san trong bo cai. Khong can cai Git, Node.js,
UXP Developer Tool, FFmpeg rieng hoac chay script PowerShell.

Neu chuc nang Video + Am thanh khong ket noi duoc FFmpeg, hay lien he nguoi
phat hanh de kiem tra native addon. HTTP bridge chi la co che du phong.
